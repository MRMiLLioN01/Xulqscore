01 — THE GAP

The file is empty. The person is not.

When an applicant has never held a loan, a card, or a registered income stream, the bureau returns a blank. The underwriter is left with a choice between two bad options: decline a creditworthy person, or approve on intuition.

XulqskorAI produces a structured, comparable signal where none existed — not a replacement for your scorecard, an additional input to it.
